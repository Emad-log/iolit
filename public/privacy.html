Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

What we collect

You pick a share tier for each batch. Pulse is structured loop stats (model, duration, tokens, tool names, error counts). Trace also includes redacted tool arguments and results. Raw also includes prompt, reply, and thinking previews. Secrets are stripped on every tier. Full file paths are scrubbed on trace and raw.

How we collect it

The Iolit client runs locally on your machine. It reads session files that already exist on disk (Claude JSONL, Cursor SQLite, Codex JSONL). It shows you the tier and the batch before sending. Nothing leaves without your explicit approval. Raw requires typing YES.

One network call

The client makes exactly one network call: sending an approved batch to iolit.dev. This is enforced by CI. The source code is public and auditable at github.com/Emad-log/iolit-client.

What happens to your data

Approved batches are stored on our servers and made available to buyers. Buyers do not receive your identity. We retain batch data while it has commercial value and delete it on request.

Your controls

  • Choose pulse, trace, or raw for each batch
  • Pause capture at any time
  • Delete previously sent batches (contact us and we'll remove them)
  • Read every line of the client before installing (MIT licensed)
  • Approve each batch individually before it's sent

Waitlist emails

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address and notify you when the product launches. We email once. We don't sell your email to anyone.
